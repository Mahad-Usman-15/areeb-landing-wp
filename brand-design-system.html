Brand Design System

Open for Business.

A system for a WordPress developer's cold-outreach landing page — built to read as credible and direct to a small business owner scrolling Facebook, not as another enterprise dev shop. Informed by Digitorm, Devsinc, 10Pearls and Folio3 (dark hero, bold geometric type, one saturated accent, pill CTAs, stat bars) — filtered through a warmer, single-operator point of view instead of their corporate blue/teal palette.

01 — Color

Ink, paper, and one signal color.

Ink
#0B1120
Hero grounds, primary text on light surfaces. Warmer than pure black.
Paper
#F6F7FA
Content section backgrounds. Cool-neutral, not cream — keeps amber the only warm note.
Signal
#E5590E
Primary CTA, stat numbers, emphasis. Trade/work-order orange — reads human, not corporate SaaS blue.
Signal Deep
#B8430A
Hover/active states, small accent text where Signal lacks contrast.
Moss
#1F8A70
Used only for "result" tags on case studies — the one place a second hue is allowed.
Slate
#545B68
Muted body copy, captions, secondary labels.
02 — Type

A blunt display face over a plain-spoken body face.

Real build: Geist Sans for display/body and Geist Mono for numbers and labels — both ship natively with Next.js via next/font, zero config, zero webfont requests. This preview uses close system-font stands-ins since this page can't load external fonts.
